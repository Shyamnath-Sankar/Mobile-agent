# Privacy Policy

**ZeroClaw Mobile** · Effective 26 October 2026

ZeroClaw Mobile ("the app") runs an AI agent on your Android phone. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: **the developer does not collect your data**. Your conversations, settings and files stay on your phone, except what you choose to send to the AI provider and services you connect.

## Who we are

The app is published on Google Play by its developer (see the contact details on the app's Google Play page). Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email listed there.

## Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

- Agent settings, agents you create, schedules, skills and procedures.
- Conversation history and the agent's memory.
- Files in the agent's workspace (files the agent or you create there).
- Files the agent makes for you (for example PDF, Word, Excel or PowerPoint files). On Android 10 and later a copy is also saved to your phone's Downloads/ZeroClaw folder, where it stays until you delete it. They leave your phone only if you share them.
- API keys and tokens you enter, stored encrypted.
- Browser data from the app's built-in browser (cookies, cache, history).
- App logs, used only for troubleshooting on the device.

The developer cannot access any of this. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDK in the app.

## Data sent off your phone

Data leaves your phone only to services you choose:

- **AI providers you configure** (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or another compatible endpoint). Your messages, the conversation context, files or images you attach, and the results of tools the agent uses are sent to that provider so it can generate replies, using the API key you supply. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that data.
- **Websites** the agent or you open in the app's browser, or that the agent fetches for research. These sites receive normal web requests from your phone, like any browser.
- **Services you connect**, such as Telegram, Discord or email, when you set up a channel or tool. Messages are exchanged with those services under their terms.
- **Reports you send** with "Report an AI response": the text you enter, the app version and the time are sent to the developer so harmful AI output can be reviewed. Reports are kept for up to 12 months.

The app does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to reach the AI providers, websites and services you choose.
- **Notifications**: to show when the agent is working, asks for your approval, or needs you in the browser.
- **Foreground service**: to keep a task you started running for a few minutes after you leave the app, or to keep the agent running if you turn on "Always on". A notification is always shown and you can stop it at any time.
- **Run at startup**: to restore scheduled jobs and "Always on" after your phone restarts.
- **Alarms & reminders** (optional, only if you allow it): to run scheduled jobs at exact times.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call logs, and does not use Accessibility services.

## Safety controls

- The agent's local server only accepts connections from this app on your phone.
- The agent asks for your approval before submitting forms, signing in, paying, typing into password or card fields, deleting or overwriting files, or running commands outside its workspace.
- Passwords are entered by you, never by the agent.

## Your choices

- Delete conversations, memories and agents in the app.
- Use "Clear data" in the Browser tab to remove browser cookies, cache and history.
- Remove an API key in the app's settings, or revoke it with your AI provider.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for use by them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the app's Google Play page, with a new effective date.
